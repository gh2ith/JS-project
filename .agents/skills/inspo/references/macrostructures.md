# Inspo Macrostructures & Taxonomy Reference

## The 19 Named Macrostructures
Inspo organizes page layouts into 19 structural archetypes:

1. **`bento-grid`**: Dense, modular card-based layout featuring distinct containers of varying sizes for features, metrics, and visuals.
2. **`long-document`**: Linear, editorial reading experience optimized for in-depth narratives, technical articles, and documentation.
3. **`marquee-hero`**: High-energy hero with continuous scrolling text ribbons, logo trains, or ticker elements.
4. **`stat-led`**: Proof-driven hierarchy placing massive numbers, performance metrics, and KPI callouts front and center.
5. **`conversational-faq`**: Question-and-answer or chat-inspired layouts emphasizing natural dialogues and customer queries.
6. **`manifesto`**: Bold typographic declaration of values, purpose, or philosophy with strong editorial pacing.
7. **`photographic`**: Image-dominant storytelling featuring full-bleed photography, art direction, and visual ambiance.
8. **`specimen`**: Type-foundry style presentation showcasing typography, UI components, or interface elements in high relief.
9. **`catalogue`**: Structured, scannable index of products, projects, or digital artifacts with filtering and clean grids.
10. **`letter`**: Personal, direct one-to-one communication format with high warmth and focused narrative.
11. **`index-first`**: Minimalist, directory-driven interface presenting a structured directory or table of contents upfront.
12. **`narrative-workflow`**: Step-by-step visual progression demonstrating how a user moves through a product or system.
13. **`split-studio`**: Asymmetric two-column composition pairing sticky contextual copy on one side with interactive/visual content on the other.
14. **`feature-stack`**: Alternating or stacked feature sections with generous vertical spacing, diagrams, and focused micro-copy.
15. **`type-specimen`**: Heavy emphasis on typefaces, font scales, weights, and character sets as the primary graphic element.
16. **`portfolio-grid`**: Visual showcase layout for creative agencies, studios, and individual practitioners.
17. **`map-diagram`**: Geospatial, topological, or node-graph centric layouts for network, logistics, or infrastructure products.
18. **`ecosystem-index`**: Comprehensive directory of integrations, community plugins, partners, or add-ons.
19. **`component-playground`**: Interactive sandbox layout inviting immediate manipulation of UI toggles, code, and live previews.

## Diversification Axes

### 1. Paper Bands (OKLCH Lightness)
- **`dark`**: Immersive dark mode surfaces.
- **`mid`**: Neutral, slate, or tinted backgrounds.
- **`light`**: Warm paper, crisp white, or light gray canvas.

### 2. Display Classes (Typography)
- `grotesk-sans` (Neo-grotesque, clean modernism)
- `geometric-sans` (Bauhaus, circular, sharp architecture)
- `roman-serif` (Editorial, literary, authoritative)
- `italic-serif` (Artisan, expressive, human)
- `slab-serif` (Sturdy, industrial, tech-forward)
- `mono` (Developer tools, terminal aesthetic, precision)
- `display-condensed-bold` (Punchy posters, high-impact titles)
- `display-heavy` (Brutalist, maximum weight)
- `system-native` (Native OS UI, seamless default)
- `handwritten` (Organic, personal, intimate)

### 3. Accent Hue Bands
- `warm` (amber, terracotta, coral, red)
- `cool` (cyan, sky blue, indigo, cobalt)
- `neutral` (monochrome, carbon, silver)
- `chromatic-other` (violet, emerald, lime)
